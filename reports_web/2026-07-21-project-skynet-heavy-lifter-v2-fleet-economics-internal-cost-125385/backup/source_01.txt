Search Query: Project SkyNet Heavy-Lifter v2 Fleet Economics: Internal Cost Structures Benchmarked Against Global Commercial Drone Delivery Standards

Altitude Limits: Delivery drones must maintain a cruising altitude between 150 and 300 feet. Flight below 150 feet is only permitted during active vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) phases.
Restricted Zones: Drones are strictly prohibited from flying over schools during operating hours, major sporting events, and government building security perimeters.
Weight Limits: The total takeoff weight (drone + payload) cannot exceed 25 lbs (11.3 kg) for unrestricted urban corridor access.
Data Retention: Flight telemetry and sensor data must be retained for a minimum of 90 days to assist in any incident investigations.

Impact on Project SkyNet
Our current "Heavy-Lifter v2" drone exceeds the 25 lbs limit by 2.5 lbs when fully loaded. We must immediately recalibrate our fleet mix, prioritizing the "Swift-Class" drones (total weight 18 lbs) for urban centers to ensure compliance. The Engineering team has been notified to modify the payload capacity limits in our dispatch software.
