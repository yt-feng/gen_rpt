# UAM Corridor Compliance Analysis for Project SkyNet

Analysis of regulatory, financial, and consumer acceptance factors affecting OmniLogistics' autonomous drone delivery initiative.

## Key Takeaways

- The Heavy-Lifter v2 drone exceeds the 25 lbs weight limit by 2.5 lbs, requiring fleet recalibration to Swift-Class drones (18 lbs) for urban centers.
- OmniLogistics is allocating $45.5M in capital expenditure for Project SkyNet, with $4.5M specifically for regulatory compliance and licensing.
- 68% of urban residents are comfortable with drone delivery, but noise (42%) and privacy (35%) are top concerns; Whisper-Prop technology and camera shuttering are recommended.
