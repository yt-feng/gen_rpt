Drones & Air Mobility | EASA

Easy Access Rules for Unmanned Aircraft Systems
Technologies for drones and electric Vertical Take-Off and Landing (eVTOL) aircraft are evolving at a rapid pace. EASA rules is ensuring that all drones and eVTOLs flying in Europe are designed to the highest safety standards.
We have today 1.6m+ registered drone operators in Europe, all governed by a single set of EU rules and authorised by the Member State authorities.
We have in Europe the most advanced rules and standards for safe and secure drone operations.
The experience of cities, operators and authorities to ensure drone operations are benefiting European citizens are growing every day.
The platform for sustainable Drone and Innovative Air Mobility in Europe bringing together all actors in the European system including cities, regions, National authorities, the EU, operators and manufactures
