TSMC's CoWoS capacity expansion and allocation has emerged as a key leading indicator for global AI GPU and AI ASIC revenue, with the company planning to expand CoWoS capacity another 80% y/y, to around 125kwpm by 2026, reinforcing its pivotal role in driving semiconductor industry capacity and revenue growth. 🛑 For every 3 US patents TSMC gains, they let 1 go TSMC ranks 2nd globally in US patent grants, trailing only Samsung. However, while securing approximately 4,200 new US patents in 2025, the semiconductor giant simultaneously allowed nearly 1,500 to lapse. Most of these assets were filed around 2015 and granted in 2017. It ultimately comes down to financial discipline. By strategically letting these patents go in 2025, TSMC saved about $6 million USD in a single year. In fact, prior to 2015, TSMC maintained most of its patents until expiration. Since then, their IP strategy has become far more agile, notably through a marked increase in the use of US Provisional Applications to secure priority. Even for an industry giant like TSMC, efficient capital allocation remains a top priority.
