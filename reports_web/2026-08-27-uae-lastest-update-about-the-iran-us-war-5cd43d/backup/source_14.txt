We have received formal notice from our Reinsurers that war risk cover will be withdrawn under the reinsurance contract that we use to protect Insureds and the Club. This action reflects a broader market response to current developments and applies to most Insurers offering Marine Liability and P&I cover, so similar notices are being issued industry-wide.
Following issuance of the notice and in accordance with the Charterers P&I Club 2025 Terms and Conditions, war risks cover will no longer respond to liabilities, losses, damages, costs or expenses for war risks that occur within the waters specifically mentioned below.
*Definition – Persian/Arabian Gulf and adjacent waters, including the Gulf of Oman and waters west of the line from Oman’s territorial limit off Cape al-Ḥadd at 22°42.5’N, 59°54.5’E northeast to the Iran-Pakistan border at 25°10.5’N, 61°37.5’E
The map below visualises the new exclusion zone, with everything to the west of the black line falling within the new exclusion zone which is defined above.
We have issued the required three days’ notice of cancellation in respect of war risks cover. Upon expiry of this period, war cover will be reinstated subject to the inclusion of a new exclusion clause, which will be added to all policies.
Please refer to the accompanying communication, which sets out the cover available for port calls through the Arabian Gulf and Persian Gulf.
The Charterers’ Liability Policy will remain in force for voyages through the excluded area, but there will be no cover for war or terrorism events that occur within Persian Gulf and Arabian Gulf.
This is a fluid and rapidly evolving situation, and further updates will follow as the situation develops. The Gulf region is currently subject to heightened hostilities and materially increased risk. Future strategy and premiums for the region will be determined by events in the days and weeks ahead.
Following recent attacks by Houthi rebels on commercial shipping in the Red Sea and Saudi Arabian Ports the reinsurance market has amended the Red Sea exclusion area that was first introduced on 20 February 2024. This impacts all P&I Insurers.
