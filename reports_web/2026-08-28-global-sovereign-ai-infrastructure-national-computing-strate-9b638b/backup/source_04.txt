Global Smart Water Management Market Projected to Reach $43.7 Billion

Global Smart Water Management Market Projected to Reach $43.7 Billion by End of 2030
Boston: According to the latest study from BCC Research, “ Smart Water Management: Global Markets to 2030 ” is expected to grow from $23.7 billion in 2025 to $43.7 billion by the end of 2030, at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 13% during the forecast period of 2025 to 2030.
This report provides a detailed analysis of the global smart water management (SWM) market, focusing on the integration of digital technologies, data analytics, and automation to optimize water usage and quality. It examines key components including hardware, software, and services, with technologies such as smart meters (AMI and AMR), sensors, and software for analytics, network monitoring, and GIS. The market is segmented by end-use sectors like utilities, commercial and industrial, and residential, and spans major global regions. Insights are offered based on industry trends, expert feedback, and data from leading water associations.
This report is particularly relevant today due to the growing global demand for clean water driven by population growth, urbanization, and industrial expansion. These pressures are prompting utilities and industries to adopt smart water management solutions that enhance efficiency and sustainability. Urban development and smart city initiatives are integrating digital technologies into water infrastructure, while industries with high water usage are investing in smart reuse and wastewater analytics to meet regulatory and environmental goals. Smart water systems also help reduce water loss through real-time monitoring and automated leak detection, improving operational performance and customer satisfaction.
The factors driving the market’s growth include:
Rising demand for clean water: As global populations grow and urban areas expand, the need for clean and safe water is becoming more urgent. Smart water management systems help ensure water quality and safety by enabling real-time monitoring and efficient treatment processes.
Water scarcity and climate change driving demand for SWM: Climate change is intensifying droughts and altering rainfall patterns, leading to water scarcity in many regions. SWM technologies offer solutions like leak detection, predictive analytics, and optimized water distribution to conserve resources and adapt to environmental changes .
Rising demand for SWM by industrial and commercial sectors: Industries and commercial enterprises are under pressure to reduce water usage and improve sustainability. Smart water systems provide tools for monitoring consumption, automating controls, and minimizing waste, helping businesses meet regulatory and environmental goals.
Advances in IoT, AI, and digital twin technology: Technological innovations are transforming water management. IoT sensors collect real-time data, AI analyzes patterns and predicts issues, and digital twins simulate water systems for better planning and maintenance, making SWM more efficient and cost-effective .
Growing urbanization and smart city initiatives: Rapid urban growth is straining traditional water infrastructure. Smart cities are integrating SWM into their frameworks to manage water resources intelligently, reduce losses, and enhance resilience, ensuring sustainable urban development .
