Regulatory Update: Urban Air Mobility (UAM) Corridors
Issued By: Legal & Compliance Department
Date: July 10, 2026
Current Status
The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) and the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) have jointly released the new "UAM Corridor Phase 1" guidelines. These guidelines dictate where and how commercial delivery drones can operate within densely populated city limits.
