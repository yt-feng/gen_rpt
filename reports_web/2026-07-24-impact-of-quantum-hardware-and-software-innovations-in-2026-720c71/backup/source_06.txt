Quantum Computing Patent Tracker 2026 | IP Leaders and Filing Trends

Tracking quantum computing intellectual property across 156 companies worldwide
What we count: Patent families, which deduplicate filings of the same invention across multiple jurisdictions. A single patent family may include US, EP, WO, CN, and JP filings. Publication counts are always higher than family counts.
Primary sources: PatentVest Quantum Computing Pulse Report (Aug 2025, 14,762 families across 1,743 assignees), euroquic.org Global Patent Landscape 2025, QED-C State of Quantum Innovation, Harrity & Harrity Patent 50 List, OECD Quantum Ecosystem Mapping 2025.
Limitations: China accounts for ~60% of all quantum patents globally but most are domestic-only filings. University patents (~37% of total) are not included in this company-focused tracker. Data is updated periodically and may not reflect the most recent filings.
Patent counts represent patent families, not individual filings. A single patent family may include multiple filings across jurisdictions. Counts are estimated from public patent databases and may not reflect unpublished or pending applications.
