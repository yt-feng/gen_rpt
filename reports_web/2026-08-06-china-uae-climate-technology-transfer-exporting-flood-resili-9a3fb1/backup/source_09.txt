Stopping natural disasters is impossible, but their impact can be significantly reduced with the right technology. Our Flood Monitoring Solution provides early detection of rising water levels through advanced sensors, telemetry systems, and intelligent data analytics. Integrated Variable Message Signs (VMS), remotely activated through a secure cloud platform, ensure rapid mass communication to the public. Flash-flood events are further validated through camera-based monitoring, enabling authorities to receive real-time alerts and take immediate action via our centralized disaster management dashboard.
iNET empowers organizations to prepare for, manage, and respond to natural disasters with confidence. Our systems deliver critical, real-time data and actionable insights that support timely and informed decision-making during emergencies.
iNET delivers a unified ecology of smart monitoring, control, and automation technologies designed to give organizations complete visibility and command. Every component engineered for precision, resilience, and real‑time performance, enabling operators to anticipate issues, optimize resources, and make confident, data‑driven decisions.
iNET supports governments, industries, and communities in assessing environmental impact and meeting regulatory compliance requirements. Our solutions enable continuous monitoring of key environmental parameters, helping stakeholders maintain sustainable and responsible operations.
iNET delivers robust cloud-based and on-premises data management solutions. Powered by the ENVAULT platform, our systems offer highly secure, mission-critical data handling tailored to the needs of government agencies, mining operations, industrial facilities, and environmental regulators.
