[PDF page 22] Key Market Drivers & Risks AI-driven demand is strong, but China’s investment cycle is cooling Market Drivers • AI Revolutionizes Equipment Demand and Advanced Packaging: AI is the strongest secular driver of semiconductor equipment demand, prompting a “supercycle” of both front-end (WFE) and back-end (packaging/testing) equipment investments globally. Emerging techniques such as 3D-IC, heterogeneous chiplet packaging (e.g., FOPLP, CoWoS/P, HBM integration), and new interconnect standards are pivotal in enabling the scaling and performance leaps required by cloud and edge AI deployments. • High-Performance Computing and Memory Transition: AI, high-performance computing, and memory transition (GAA, HBM, 3D stacking) are expected to drive multi-year equipment and infrastructure investment. Risks • Potential Slowdown of AI Investment and Adoption: The slowdown in AI investment and adoption poses a significant risk. • US Tariffs and Export Controls: US tariffs and export controls, along with the “China-for-China” manufacturing strategy, create uncertainties and drive regional supply chain shifts. Potential US tariffs exceeding 100% on imported chips and other items fuel uncertainty, with details pending. • Regional Supply Chain Shifts: Companies with US plants, such as TSMC, Samsung, Amkor and GlobalWafers, are seen as better shielded from risks compared to mature-node Asia foundries and OSATs.
