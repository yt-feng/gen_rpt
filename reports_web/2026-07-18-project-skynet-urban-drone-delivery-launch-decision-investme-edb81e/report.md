# Project SkyNet Urban Drone Delivery Launch Decision: Investment Case, Consumer Adoption, UAM Corridor Compliance, and Heavy-Lifter.

Analysis of OmniLogistics' $45.5M drone delivery initiative, regulatory compliance, consumer acceptance, and fleet recalibration.

## Key Takeaways

- OmniLogistics is allocating $45.5M in capital expenditure for Project SkyNet, a 25% increase from Q1 projections.
- 68% of urban residents are comfortable with drone delivery, but 42% cite noise as the top concern; Whisper-Prop technology reduces decibels by 40%.
- Heavy-Lifter v2 exceeds the 25 lbs weight limit by 2.5 lbs when fully loaded; Swift-Class drones (18 lbs) must be prioritized for urban centers.
