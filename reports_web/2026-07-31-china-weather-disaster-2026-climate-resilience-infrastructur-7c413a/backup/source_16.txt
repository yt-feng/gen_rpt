The increasing deployment of connected water management technologies is enabling continuous monitoring of water quality, pressure, leakage, and consumption patterns. These solutions provide utilities with actionable insights that support predictive maintenance, faster leak detection, demand forecasting, and efficient resource allocation. Improved data-driven decision-making is helping reduce water losses, lower operating costs, and strengthen service reliability. As governments continue to prioritize sustainable water management and smart city initiatives, the adoption of smart water management platforms is expected to expand steadily across both developed and emerging economies.
By hardware, the smart water meter segment is estimated to hold the largest market share during the forecast period.
The smart water meter segment is expected to account for the largest share of the global Smart Water Management Market during the forecast period, driven by the increasing need for accurate water consumption monitoring, non-revenue water (NRW) reduction, and improved utility billing efficiency. Water utilities are rapidly replacing conventional meters with Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) and Advanced Metering Reading (AMR) solutions that enable real-time data collection, remote meter reading, leak detection, and consumption analytics. Rising investments in smart city projects, aging water infrastructure modernization, and government initiatives promoting water conservation are further accelerating the deployment of smart water meters across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors. These devices help utilities enhance operational visibility, reduce manual meter-reading costs, improve billing accuracy, detect unauthorized consumption, and support predictive maintenance. The integration of IoT connectivity, cloud platforms, and AI-powered analytics enables utilities to optimize water distribution, respond quickly to network issues, and improve customer engagement through usage insights. As water scarcity, regulatory compliance requirements, and digital transformation initiatives continue to intensify worldwide, smart water meters are becoming a critical hardware component for enabling efficient, data-driven water management, supporting sustainable resource utilization, and strengthening the long-term resilience of water utility networks.

By services, the managed services segment is projected to witness the higher growth rate during the forecast period.
The managed services segment is expected to witness the highest growth rate as water utilities increasingly adopt continuous monitoring, remote infrastructure management, and predictive maintenance to improve operational efficiency. Aging water infrastructure, rising non-revenue water losses, and the growing deployment of smart water technologies are driving demand for outsourced services that ensure reliable system performance while reducing operational complexity. Managed service providers deliver real-time monitoring, cybersecurity, software updates, and data management, enabling utilities to improve reliability while focusing on core operations. Managed services also accelerate digital transformation through cloud-based analytics, lifecycle management, and continuous system optimization. As utilities expand smart water projects, these services enable seamless technology integration, regulatory compliance, and improved asset performance without significant in-house investments. The increasing adoption of AI-driven leak detection, cloud platforms, and remote asset monitoring is further strengthening operational resilience, enhancing customer service, and driving sustained growth of managed services in the Smart Water Management Market.

Asia Pacific is projected to register the highest growth rate in the Smart Water Management Market during the forecast period, driven by rapid urbanization, increasing water scarcity, aging water infrastructure, and strong government investments in digital water technologies across countries such as China, India, Japan, Singapore, and Australia.
