Consumer Acceptance Survey: Urban Drone Delivery
Study Period: May - June 2026
Sample Size: 5,200 urban residents across New York, London, Tokyo, Singapore, and Dubai.
Key Findings

Overall Acceptance: 68% of respondents indicated they are "comfortable" or "very comfortable" receiving small packages via drone.
Primary Concerns:
Noise Pollution: 42% cited the buzzing sound of drones as their top concern.
Privacy: 35% were worried about cameras on drones recording private property.
Safety: 18% expressed fear of packages being dropped or drones malfunctioning over crowded areas.
Willingness to Pay: 55% of users are willing to pay a premium of up to $2.50 for drone delivery if it guarantees arrival within 30 minutes.
